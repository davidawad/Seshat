Correct answer

CN VII (Facial nerve) — Bell's palsy

Why

Complete lower-motor-neuron facial weakness — forehead included — localizes to CN VII itself, not a central lesion. A UMN facial palsy spares the forehead, since the frontalis gets bilateral cortical input. Bell's palsy accounts for roughly 60–75% of acute unilateral facial palsy presentations.

Source — Baugh RF et al., Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg. 2013;149(3S):S1–S27 (AAO-HNS Clinical Practice Guideline: Bell's Palsy)
