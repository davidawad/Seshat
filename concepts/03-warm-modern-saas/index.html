You said: "the Pike balancing test, weighing the burden against the benefit"

Correct answer

The Pike balancing test — weigh the law's burden on interstate commerce against its local benefits.

If the burden is "clearly excessive in relation to the putative local benefits," the law falls — even without facial discrimination against out-of-staters.[1] Courts apply this same balancing test outside the discrimination context, which is what usually trips people up on the exam.

[1]Pike v. Bruce Church, Inc., 397 U.S. 137, 142 (1970)
