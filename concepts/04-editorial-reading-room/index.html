III.

Calibration

Confidence is a prediction. This is the record of how often it’s right.

Every time you answer, Seshat asks how sure you were, then quietly checks whether that certainty was earned. The gap between the two is the whole point of the table below — most people are worse calibrated than they think, especially in the row they’d bet money on.
