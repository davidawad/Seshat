Correct answer Cuneiform

Cuneiform began as pictographic tallies for grain and livestock. Scribes gradually simplified the pictures into wedge-shaped strokes and started using them for sound as well as meaning — that shift, from pictures to phonetics, is what let clay tablets hold full sentences instead of just counts.

✎ Denise Schmandt-Besserat, How Writing Came About, University of Texas Press, 1996, ch. 6.
