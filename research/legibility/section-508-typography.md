# U.S. Section 508 — Fonts & Typography Guidance

**Citation:** Section508.gov (U.S. General Services Administration). Fonts & Typography (accessibility development guidance).

**Link:** https://www.section508.gov/develop/fonts-typography/

## Summary

The guidance recommends sans-serif fonts for body/continuous-reading text on screen, stating that for people with low vision "serifs significantly degrade legibility," while allowing serif use for headings/emphasis; it sets a hard minimum print/physical size of 3/16 inch (4.8mm, based on uppercase "I" height, roughly 16pt) only for contexts where users cannot adjust size, but for regular digital content (where zoom/resizing is available) recommends practical sizes of 11-12pt/15-16px for web/document body text and warns against running text below 9pt. It also calls for maintaining at least 4.5:1 contrast, supporting 200% text resize without loss of function, using real text rather than text-as-image, and avoiding layouts that require horizontal scrolling. For Seshat, whose body size range (11.5-13pt) falls within the recommended 11-12pt+ band and which offers Atkinson Hyperlegible/Verdana/Inter as sans-serif options alongside serif choices (Source Serif 4, Georgia), the guidance suggests sans-serif should probably be the app's *default* recommendation even though serif options remain available as user preference.
