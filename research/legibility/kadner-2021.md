# AdaptiFont: Increasing Individuals' Reading Speed with a Generative Font Model and Bayesian Optimization

**Citation:** Kadner, F., Keller, Y., & Rothkopf, C. A. (2021). AdaptiFont: Increasing Individuals' Reading Speed with a Generative Font Model and Bayesian Optimization. In *Proceedings of the 2021 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '21)*.

**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.10741

## Summary

Fetched successfully (arXiv preprint abstract). AdaptiFont uses non-negative matrix factorization over a set of existing typefaces to build a continuous, generative "font space," then applies active learning and Bayesian optimization in a human-in-the-loop process to converge on a custom font tailored to each individual's reading speed. The system found font configurations that meaningfully improved reading speed relative to baseline, and the optimized fonts differed significantly across individual readers, demonstrating that typography can be personalized systematically rather than by fixed preset alone. For Seshat, this is corroborating evidence (alongside Wallace et al.) that per-user font optimization has real effect on reading speed; while Seshat offers a fixed preset menu rather than a generative/adaptive font, the underlying finding — individualized typeface choice measurably affects reading speed — supports the design decision to expose multiple typeface options to users.
